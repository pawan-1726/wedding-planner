Our Story

A Decade of Crafting Unforgettable Weddings

Founded in 2012, EverAfter began as a two-person studio with a simple belief: every couple deserves a wedding as unique as their love. Today, our team of 20+ planners has orchestrated celebrations across 15 countries.

We combine meticulous planning with artistic vision, ensuring every detail — from the first invitation to the last dance — is thoughtfully curated.
