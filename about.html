Who We Are

A Studio Built on Love and Precision

EverAfter was founded by Amelia Rose, a visionary planner who believed weddings should be as individual as the couples they celebrate. What began in a small New York studio has grown into an internationally recognized team of 20+ planners, designers, and coordinators.

We've orchestrated over 540 weddings across 15 countries — from intimate garden ceremonies to multi-day cultural celebrations. Our approach blends meticulous project management with artistic design, ensuring no detail is overlooked and no moment is missed.
