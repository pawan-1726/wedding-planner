What We Offer

End-to-End Wedding Planning

From the first spark of an idea to the final farewell, our services cover every aspect of your celebration.

Full-Service Planning

Complete planning from engagement to "I do" — budget management, vendor coordination, guest logistics, and day-of execution.

Design & Styling

Concept development, color stories, floral direction, lighting design, and decor curation that bring your vision to life.

Venue Sourcing

Curated venue recommendations, site visits, contract negotiation, and capacity planning to find the perfect backdrop.

Day-Of Coordination

For couples who planned their own wedding — we take over on the big day so you and your family can be fully present.

Destination Weddings

Multi-day celebrations in breathtaking locations worldwide — travel logistics, local vendors, and guest experiences handled.

Vendor Management

Photographers, videographers, caterers, musicians, and more — sourced, vetted, and coordinated by our team.
